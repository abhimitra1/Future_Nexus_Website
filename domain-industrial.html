Teaching Philosophy
Industry 5.0 Pedagogy · GoToMarket in 100 Days
GATE-Driven Progression

No phase advancement without clearing each GATE checkpoint — quality is enforced by process, not just grading.

PPR Framework First

Every project starts by defining its Product, Process, and Resources — before any CATIA sketch is drawn.

Digital Before Physical

Validate fully in SIMULIA — structural, aerodynamic, thermal — before any physical prototype is commissioned.

Demonstrate Then Design

Every lab begins with a live instructor walkthrough on the DS platform. Students replicate before they create.

Min & Stretch Goals

Every milestone specifies a minimum deliverable and a stretch target — no one stalls, no one is left unchallenged.

Team-Based Projects

2–4 person teams throughout. Shared accountability, defined roles, and real collaboration — not solo assignments.

IPR from Day 1

Every project is scoped to identify patentable innovations. Prior art search and patent claim drafting are built into CUGM2143.

Go Green by Default

EVs, agri-drones, and solar-integrated products are prioritised. Sustainability is a design constraint, not a bonus.

Industry-Scale Problems

No toy projects. Every capstone is a real-world product brief — designed, simulated, and digitally launched for market.

Phase 1
2 Courses · 11 Credits · Days 1–50
Foundation — Design Thinking & Tools
CUGM2140

Design Thinking & Innovation Through GATE Process

1-2-0 · 3 cr
Design Process + GTM Methodology · Days 1–25
  • Human-centred design: empathise → define → ideate → prototype → test
  • PPR framework — defining Product, Process, and Resources for any brief
  • Market analysis: competitive benchmarking, SWOT, and customer requirements
  • GATE 0 deliverable: converting customer requirements to design parameters
  • 3DExperience platform onboarding: dashboards, roles, and first CATIA sketch
  • GTM planning: product pitch structure, value proposition, and roadmap creation
Prereq: Basic engineering drawing literacy
CUGM2141

PLM Tools on Dassault Platform — DYMOLA, CATIA & SIMULIA

2-6-0 · 8 cr
CATIA + SIMULIA + DYMOLA · Days 5–50
  • DYMOLA: physics-based system modelling — powertrain, suspension, and structural parameter optimisation
  • CATIA Part Design: parametric solid modelling, GD&T, tolerancing, and feature history
  • CATIA Assembly Design: constraint-based assemblies, motion studies, and BOM generation
  • CATIA Generative Surface Design: Class A surfacing, skinning, and aesthetic styling
  • SIMULIA Abaqus: FEA structural stress, modal analysis, and fatigue life prediction
  • SIMULIA CFD: aerodynamic drag, thermal management, and fluid flow simulation
  • Digital Twin creation: linking CATIA geometry to live SIMULIA behaviour models
  • Gate 2–3 deliverables: full product drawing package + digital validation report
Prereq: CUGM2140
Phase 2
1 Course · 3 Credits · Days 51–75
Process Management & PLM
CUGM2142

Process Management Using ENOVIA

1-2-0 · 3 cr
PLM + BOM + Process Engineering · Days 51–75
  • ENOVIA: product data management, revision control, and ECO/ECN change workflows
  • Bill of Materials (BOM): EBOM and MBOM hierarchy, sub-assembly structure, make-or-buy decisions
  • DELMIA Process Planner: manufacturing sequence simulation, ergonomics analysis, and cycle time optimisation
  • Vendor development: supplier qualification criteria, RFQ process, and cost benchmarking methodology
  • Gate 4–5 deliverables: regulatory certification checklist, production planning, and vendor list
  • Project governance: risk register, weekly review cadence, milestone tracking, and documentation standards
Prereq: CUGM2141
Capstone
CUGM2143  ·  0-0-8  ·  8 Credits  ·  Days 76–100

Product Development — GoToMarket in 100 Days

Teams of 2–4 take a product from brief to digital launch in 25 days of intensive project work. Plan, design, simulate, validate, and present a working digital product to a faculty panel and Dassault Systèmes industry reviewers. All tracks require a complete 3DX model, SIMULIA validation report, BOM, and a live product pitch.

A Electric Vehicle

E-bike or e-scooter: powertrain in DYMOLA, full body in CATIA, validated in SIMULIA

B E-Rickshaw

800kg 4+1 passenger carrier: chassis, suspension, brakes, CATIA assembly + FEA

C Agri Drone (UAV)

Quadrotor for pesticide spraying or surveillance: CFD aerodynamics + structural load analysis

D Humanoid Robot

Robotic arm or torso: kinematics, actuation, motion simulation in CATIA Kinematics

E AgriBot

Ground-based autonomous agricultural robot: locomotion design, CAD assembly, BOM

F Industrial Machine

3D printer, pick-and-place robot cell, or CNC redesign: full mechanical + controls spec

G Smart Mobility

Novel micro-mobility concept: ideation through GATE 0–3, full digital prototype and pitch
