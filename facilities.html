GTM XR Lab

Odisha's Most Advanced
XR Research Facility

A full-spectrum Extended Reality complex spanning three dedicated zones — purpose-built for research, development, and immersive experience. The GTM XR Lab is where IFN's XR programme comes to life, giving students access to professional-grade hardware and software from their first semester.

Lab Zones

Experience Zone

A dedicated immersive showcase space equipped with Meta Quest 3, HoloLens 2, and Varjo headsets. Used for application demos, client walkthroughs, and user testing sessions with student-built XR applications.

Thinkers Area

An open ideation and collaboration studio — whiteboards, display walls, and flexible seating for concept mapping, storyboarding, and design critique. Where spatial problems are solved before a single line of code is written.

Development & Testing

High-performance workstations with RTX GPUs running Unity and Unreal Engine. Device profiling rigs, performance benchmarking tools, and SDK integration environments for iterative XR development and testing.

Infrastructure

Testnet Node Cluster

Private Ethereum (Hardhat/Foundry), Avalanche Fuji testnet, and Hyperledger Fabric nodes — always running, accessible to all enrolled students via secure key-pair authentication.

Protocol Dev Workstations

Dedicated development machines with full Ethereum toolchain (Foundry, Hardhat, Ethers.js, Wagmi), smart contract auditing tools, and on-chain monitoring dashboards.

Security Audit Station

Isolated environment for smart contract security research — Slither, Mythril, and manual audit workflows. Students learn to identify and remediate vulnerabilities in production contract code.
