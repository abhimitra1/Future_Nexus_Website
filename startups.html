Innovation & Venture Ecosystem

From Research
to Real-World Value

The Centre helps teams move from insight to validated solution by combining applied research, technical mentorship, specialist labs, industry access, and venture support.

We believe the most powerful learning happens when students have skin in the game. IFN's ecosystem provides lab access, mentorship from active industry practitioners, and a tightly-knit community of builders — the same infrastructure that has already produced startups in on-chain payments, precision agriculture drones, XR technology, agri-commerce, and developer education. These aren't side projects. They are real companies, built inside these labs, by students who graduate as founders.
