World-Class Facilities

Every lab is built to production standards — because practice on real tools is the only preparation that matters.

GTM XR Lab

Full-spectrum XR complex with dedicated zones for immersive experience, collaborative thinking, and hands-on development & testing.

Industrial Design Lab

Hands-on product design studio built for end-to-end workflows — from concept sketching and CAD to physical fabrication and finishing.

Blockchain Lab

Private Ethereum and Hyperledger testnet nodes with smart contract deployment infrastructure and real-time on-chain analytics.

Immersive demo space with Meta Quest 3, HoloLens 2, and Varjo headsets — built for application showcase, client walkthroughs, and user testing.
